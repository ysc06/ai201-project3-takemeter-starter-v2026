## Candidate distinctions

Re-read pass, 2026-11-22. Each one is something you can point at in the text.

1. **Some posts back a claim with a number or checkable fact. Most don't.**
   - Seen in about 7 / 58: #2 (two named game results), #11 (three 1-point games),
     #15 (tied 4th in the East, 82-game season), #41 (70 FTs), #44 (1 week,
     hamstring), #45 (30 fouls by half), #42 (Duren out, a checkable fact)
   - Test: could you look it up and be wrong? "Tied for fourth" yes; "bums" no.
   - Trap: numbers in jokes. #33, #35, #40, #54 cite real records ("6-2",
     "7-2") but pin them on a statue or a meme. The number has to support the
     claim, not decorate a punchline.

2. **Some posts give a reason. Most stop at the verdict.**
   - With a reason (about 6 / 58): #3 (too few games to judge), #4 (still
     winning without Ant), #30 (karma rewards cathartic takes), #42 (no Duren
     → weak paint D → fouls), #56 (Doc's comments fired the team up, a guess)
   - Bare verdict: #10, #20 ("best coach in the league"), #21, #27, #55
   - Note: a reason is not the same as a fact. #56 has a reason and no
     evidence; #41 has a fact and no argument. Distinctions 1 and 2 overlap
     but are not the same line.

3. **Some posts talk about the game. A big cluster talks about the sub.**
   - About the sub (about 12 / 58): #3, #17, #18, #19, #25, #27, #28, #29, #30,
     #55 (half of it)
   - These are almost all about overreacting. The community has its own word
     for a bad take: overreaction, "First Take" (#25), "a narrative based on
     whatever happened 5 minutes ago" (#28). That vocabulary is worth using.
   - Off-topic logistics are separate and easy to spot: broadcast (#7, #13),
     schedule (#12), mods (#14), thread setup (#1).

4. **Some posts are sincere. Many are jokes or hype that only look like claims.**
   - Joke with a fake cause (a recognizable form, "X since Y"): #33, #35, #50,
     #51, #54
   - Parody or sarcasm: #17, #31, #52, #57, #58
   - Hype or rooting, no claim at all: #5, #6, #8, #46, #47, #49
   - Together about 20 / 58, the largest group. Whether these are a label
     (`reaction`?) or out of scope decides what you collect.

Also noticed, for collection rather than labels:
- About 10 / 58 mean nothing without the parent comment (#22, #34, #37, #48,
  #53). The model only sees one post, so these are noise either way.
- #33 and #54 are the same joke. Near-duplicates leak across splits.
- Posts that clear distinction 1 are about 1 in 8. A label built on it will be
  the thin one; plan to collect from post-game threads, where #41–#45 came from.
