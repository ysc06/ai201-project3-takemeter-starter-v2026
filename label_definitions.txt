analysis: a claim about basketball backed by at least one specific, checkable fact (a stat, a record, a standing, a named result, a lineup or injury status, or a quote) offered as support for the claim
hot_take: a confident claim about basketball (a verdict, ranking or prediction) stated with no checkable support; a reason with no fact behind it still counts as no support
reaction: an immediate emotional or joking response to a game or moment (hype, rooting, sarcasm, a meme) that makes no claim beyond what just happened
